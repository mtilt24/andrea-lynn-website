An 8-Week Women’s Journey

The Threshold

There are moments when the familiar structures of our lives begin to fall away.

A marriage ends. A relationship changes. A chapter closes. An identity we’ve carried for years no longer fits. Or a deeper calling within us begins asking for something more.

And somewhere in the uncertainty, a question emerges:

Who am I now, and what do I truly desire?

REBUILD is an eight-week transformational journey for women who are ready to turn life’s transitions into an opportunity for self-discovery, reclamation, and conscious reinvention.

Not by rushing into what’s next, but by turning inward, honoring what has been, releasing what no longer belongs, and reconnecting with the woman beneath the roles, expectations, and conditioning.

The Invitation

What if the ending is also an initiation?

When life changes, we often rush to figure out what comes next. We make plans and try to rebuild from the same identities and patterns that shaped the life we’re leaving behind. But what if this transition is asking you to meet yourself more fully?

REBUILD invites you to reclaim the parts of yourself you’ve silenced or forgotten, remember that your desires matter, and begin cultivating trust in your own knowing.

You don’t have to navigate the passage alone.

The Journey

Five Gateways.

Inspired by the symbolic journey of Inanna’s Descent, REBUILD moves through five gateways:

  1. i.Self-Acceptance
  2. ii.Forgiveness
  3. iii.Gratitude
  4. iv.Freedom
  5. v.Love

Each gateway creates space for somatic inquiry, breathwork, embodied practices, reflection, and supportive connection. The mythology provides an evocative framework; the practical objective is greater self-trust, freedom, and agency.

Is This You?

Who This Is For.

For women standing at a threshold after divorce or relationship change, bereavement or other loss, an empty nest, career shifts, identity changes, or an inner call for something more. You may still be navigating uncertainty, but you are ready to actively participate in your next chapter.

  • You’re ready to deepen self-acceptance and trust your inner guidance.
  • You recognize patterns and conditioning that no longer serve you.
  • You want to reconnect with your body, desires, pleasure, and voice.
  • You value supportive community alongside personal inquiry and embodiment.
  • You’re open to reflective work and guided practices between calls.

The Possibility

What Becomes Possible.

Greater self-compassion, insight into inherited patterns, a more trusting relationship with your body and emotions, clarity around desire and choice, stronger boundaries, and a more intentional relationship with what comes next.

This is about reclaiming authorship of your life.
