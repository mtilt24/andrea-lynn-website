A Free Practice

Come out of your head. Come back into your body.

Vitality Awakening is a 10-minute guided breath, sound and movement practice to awaken the energy that is already there.

Through breath, sound, and intuitive movement, you'll be invited to soften what's holding, create space in your body, and allow more of your natural life-force energy to move through you.

No spam. Just real tools and occasional love notes from me.

What It Does

10 minutes to breathe, move + feel

You can begin with a particular place in your body that feels tight, heavy, numb, restless, or stagnant. Or simply come in curious about what wants to move.

  • Release physical and emotional tension
  • Shift out of mental overactivity and into your body
  • Move stagnant or restless energy
  • Create more openness through the chest and heart space
  • Reconnect with pleasure and sensation
  • Allow sexual and creative energy to move more freely
  • Cultivate a greater sense of peace, openness, and aliveness

You don't need any special equipment or previous breathwork experience. Simply arrive as you are.

Your body knows how to move toward aliveness

Sometimes it simply needs the space, breath, and permission to do it.
