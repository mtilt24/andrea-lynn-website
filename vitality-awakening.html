Vitality Awakening

A 10-Minute Breath, Sound + Movement Practice

Come out of your head. Come back into your body. Awaken the energy that's already there.

Vitality Awakening is a guided somatic breathwork practice designed to help you move stagnant energy, release built-up tension, and reconnect with your natural sense of aliveness.

Through breath, sound, and intuitive movement, you'll be invited to soften what's holding, create space in your body, and allow more of your natural life-force energy to move through you.

Before You Begin

What You'll Need

Create a space where you can fully let go and be with yourself for the next 10 to 15 minutes.

  • A comfortable place to sit or lie down
  • A quiet space where you won't be disturbed
  • Comfortable clothing that allows you to breathe and move freely
  • An eye mask, if you'd like to turn your attention more deeply inward
  • A journal and pen nearby for anything you'd like to capture afterward
  • A few minutes after the practice to rest, integrate, and notice what has shifted

You don't need any special equipment or previous breathwork experience.

Simply arrive as you are.

A Note on Safety + Contraindications

Please Read Before Practicing

Vitality Awakening includes periods of faster, deeper, activating breathing. This type of breathing may create temporary sensations such as tingling, lightheadedness, changes in body temperature, muscle tension, or emotional intensity.

This activating breathwork practice is not recommended if you are pregnant or if you have uncontrolled high blood pressure, significant cardiovascular disease, a history of aneurysm or stroke, epilepsy or a seizure disorder, glaucoma or retinal detachment, or another serious medical condition for which intense breathing could pose a risk.

If you have a significant medical or psychiatric condition, have recently undergone surgery, or are unsure whether activating breathwork is appropriate for you, consult a qualified healthcare professional before participating.

Never practice activating breathwork while driving, in or near water, or anywhere that becoming lightheaded could put you at risk of injury.

Listen to Your Body

Breathwork is not about pushing through.

You are always welcome to slow your breath, decrease the intensity, keep your eyes open, rest, or stop the practice completely.

If at any point you feel faint, experience significant pain, severe shortness of breath, chest pain, or feel unsafe, stop the activating breathing and return to your natural breath. Seek appropriate medical attention for severe or persistent symptoms.

There is nothing you need to force. Let your body set the pace.

Your Vitality Awakening Practice

10 Minutes to Breathe, Move + Feel

You can begin this practice with a particular place in your body that feels tight, heavy, numb, restless, or stagnant.

Or simply come in curious about what wants to move.

During the practice, I'll guide you through activating breath, sound, and intuitive movement while demonstrating the process alongside you.

This Practice Is Designed To Help You

  • Release physical and emotional tension
  • Shift out of mental overactivity and into your body
  • Move stagnant or restless energy
  • Create more openness through the chest and heart space
  • Reconnect with pleasure and sensation
  • Allow sexual and creative energy to move more freely
  • Cultivate a greater sense of peace, openness, and aliveness

Your experience may be subtle or powerful. You may notice energy, emotion, warmth, tingling, movement, laughter, tears, stillness, or nothing dramatic at all.

There is no correct experience. Let your body lead.

Find a comfortable position before you press play.

After Your Practice

Give Yourself a Moment to Notice

Before moving immediately into the rest of your day, give yourself a moment to simply notice.

What feels different?

Where is there more space?

What can you feel now that you couldn't feel before?

Did anything surprise you?

Is there anything your body seems to be asking for now?

If you have your journal nearby, take a few minutes to write without trying to analyze or make sense of what happened.

You don't have to understand the experience. Just notice what has shifted.

Your body knows how to move toward aliveness. Sometimes it simply needs the space, breath, and permission to do it.
